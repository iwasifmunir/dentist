Ask about implants
What it is

A replacement root, not just a replacement tooth

A bridge grinds down the healthy teeth on either side of the gap. A denture sits on the gum and moves. An implant does neither: a titanium post is placed into the jawbone where the root used to be, the bone grows onto it, and a crown is fixed on top. It is the only option that stops the jawbone from shrinking where the tooth was lost.

Who it suits

  • One missing tooth, or several, where the neighbouring teeth are healthy
  • A failing tooth that cannot be saved by a root canal
  • Denture wearers who want something fixed rather than removable
  • Anyone with enough jawbone — and if there is not enough, a graft usually solves it

What it costs

Priced after the X-ray

We do not quote implants over the phone, because the number depends on your bone. What we do promise: the full figure in writing at the consultation, before any treatment starts, and not a rupee added afterwards.

The quote includes the implant, the abutment, the porcelain crown, every review appointment and a five-year written warranty.

What can add to it: a bone graft or a sinus lift, if the X-ray shows you need one. You are told at the consultation, not halfway through.

Instalments: larger plans can be split into three monthly payments, interest free, with no third-party financing.

How it goes, step by step

  1. Consultation and X-ray (free, 30 minutes). We check bone height and density and tell you honestly whether an implant is the right answer.
  2. Placement (60–90 minutes). Local anaesthetic. You feel pressure, not pain. Most people go back to work the next day.
  3. Healing (3–4 months). The bone fuses to the implant. You wear a temporary tooth in the gap so the space is never visible.
  4. The crown (2 visits). We take a digital scan, match the shade to your own teeth, and fit the final crown.
  5. Reviews. Three follow-ups over the first year, included.

Recovery — honestly

Expect mild swelling for two to three days and some tenderness for about a week. Painkillers you can buy over the counter are usually enough. Soft food for a few days, no smoking for at least two weeks — smoking is the single biggest cause of implant failure, and we will tell you that plainly rather than take your money and hope.

How long they last

Well-placed implants in a healthy mouth routinely last 15 to 25 years, and often for life. The crown on top may need replacing after 10 to 15 years, which is a much smaller job. What shortens that: smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, and skipping cleanings.

Implant FAQ

The questions we get asked most

Does getting an implant hurt?

The placement itself does not — the area is fully numb, and most patients say it was easier than the extraction that preceded it. Afterwards, expect soreness rather than pain for about a week.

How long before I have a proper tooth?

Three to four months from placement to final crown, because the bone needs that time to fuse. You are never without a tooth in the gap — a temporary is fitted the same day.

Is an implant better than a bridge?

Usually, yes. A bridge is faster and cheaper up front but requires cutting down two healthy teeth, and it does not stop bone loss. Over 15 years an implant tends to work out cheaper because bridges need replacing.

What if I do not have enough bone?

A bone graft rebuilds the site and adds three to six months to the timeline. It is routine, done under local anaesthetic, and we will tell you at the consultation whether you need one.

Can I get several implants at once?

Yes. For multiple missing teeth we often place two implants and bridge between them, which costs less than one implant per tooth. For a full arch, four to six implants can support a fixed set.
